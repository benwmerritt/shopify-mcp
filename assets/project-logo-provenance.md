# Shopify logo provenance

`project-logo.svg` uses the inspected Simple Icons Shopify shopping-bag path, with its published brand colour, #7AB55C. The geometry is unchanged. `project-logo.png` is a 256 x 256 transparent raster of that SVG, rendered with librsvg and Cairo.

Source pinned to Simple Icons commit 98820a4dc8c363ca72fa2c0d294ea4a0a9bba75d, retrieved 2026-10-06:
